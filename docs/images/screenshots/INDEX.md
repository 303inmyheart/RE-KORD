## Notes

- The data is a copy of a real music library. Covers were applied with Studio › Covers, and only where the match was clear. Genres and dates come from the Studio metadata fetch, and lyrics from LRCLIB.
- Favorites, moods, playlists, play counts and Plectr records are showcase data on the Default account.
- The Admin › Status and Library shots show the local folder paths as `/srv/music` and `/var/lib/rekord`.
- The custom-theme background is a generated abstract gradient.
